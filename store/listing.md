## Name
DARKWEB

## Summary (≤132 chars)
Plunge any page into total darkness. Your mouse becomes a flashlight — move it to see. With a spooky horror drone. A joke.

## Category
Fun / Entertainment

## Description
Lights out.

Flip DARKWEB on and the page you're reading drops into total darkness. The only
way to see anything is to move your mouse — it carries a small soft flashlight
halo, like a torch in a pitch-black room. A low, cinematic horror drone hums
quietly in the background for maximum dread.

The page stays completely usable underneath the dark: you can still scroll,
click links, and type. It's a useless, delightful way to browse the web.

FEATURES
• Move your mouse to reveal the page under a soft flashlight beam.
• Torch flicker — the beam breathes like a real flame (toggle off if you like).
• Adjustable halo size and darkness.
• A spooky background drone, synthesised on the fly. Volume kept low; turn it off
  anytime.
• Three ways to exit: the toolbar switch, the Ctrl+Shift+K / ⌘⇧K hotkey, or Esc.

PRIVACY
DARKWEB collects nothing, sends nothing, and makes zero network requests. Its
only permission is "storage", used to remember your settings on your own device.

Just a joke. Enjoy the dark.

## Permission justification
- **storage** — remembers your on/off state and slider settings on this device.
  No data leaves your machine.
- **Host permission `<all_urls>` (content script)** — the blackout has to run on
  whatever page you're viewing; that's the whole point. No page data is read,
  stored, or transmitted — the extension only paints an overlay on top.

## Single purpose
A novelty extension that darkens the current web page and reveals it under a
mouse-following flashlight halo, with an optional ambient horror drone.
