DARKWEB — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

DARKWEB collects no data whatsoever.

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or page content.
  • It makes no network requests of any kind.
  • Its only Chrome permission is storage, used solely to remember your on/off state and display settings (halo size, darkness, flicker, music, volume) on your own device. This data never leaves your computer.
  • The background sound is generated on your device with the Web Audio API; no audio is downloaded or streamed.
